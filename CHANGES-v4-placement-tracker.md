## New features

1. **Send Resume & Analysis** (student). Upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT file. The server scores it out of 100, gives an ATS match, finds sections and skills, and suggests fixes. Every upload is saved as a new version.
2. **Placement Q&A Test** (student). 10 MCQs per test from 39 placement questions (aptitude, reasoning, English, CSE, Civil, HR). Answers are graded on the server. Attempted / correct / wrong / skipped is saved for every test.
3. **AI Mock Interview** (student). 5 questions per round in HR, CSE or Civil. You can type or speak the answers. Gemini scores them when `GEMINI_API_KEY` is set; otherwise an offline auto-scorer does. Each round gives an overall %, tech / communication / confidence ratings and a verdict.
4. **Faculty Notices** (faculty/admin send, student completes). Pending students get an automatic reminder every N minutes, up to 3. After the 3rd, the student's portal **locks** until they complete the notice. Faculty can send "Remind" or "Unlock" manually.
5. **Student Placement Tracker** (faculty + admin). Has 6 tabs: all students table, top resumes ranking, resumes received (who sent which, with download), Q&A analysis, mock interviews, and notice status. It refreshes every 20 s.
